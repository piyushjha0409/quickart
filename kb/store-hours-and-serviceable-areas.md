## Dark stores in Bengaluru

| Store | Store ID | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Koramangala | blr_koramangala_02 | 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM |
| Indiranagar | blr_indiranagar_01 | 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM |
| HSR Layout | blr_hsr_01 | 6:00 AM to 1:00 AM |
| Whitefield | blr_whitefield_01 | 6:00 AM to 12:00 midnight |

Stores open every day, including public holidays. Hours may be shortened during citywide events or severe weather; the app shows the store as closed when it is not taking orders.

## Delivery area

Each store delivers within about 3 km of the store. The customer is served by the nearest open store that covers their location. When the app says "Not serviceable", there is no store covering that location at the moment.

## Coverage changes

Coverage can shrink temporarily when rider availability is low or roads are blocked. Customers can still browse but cannot check out until a store is available. QuickCart does not take scheduled or pre-orders.
