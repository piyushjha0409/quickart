## What counts as missing

An item is missing when it was charged on the order but not in the delivered bags. This includes partially missing quantities, for example 2 packets ordered and 1 delivered.

## Before raising it

Support should first check the order for:
- **Out-of-stock removals:** items removed before packing are not charged and appear as "Removed — out of stock" on the order. These are not missing.
- **Substitutions:** the item may have been replaced with a substitute (see "Substitution policy").
- **Split deliveries:** large orders occasionally arrive in two bags or with two riders a few minutes apart. The order timeline shows this.

## Resolution

If the item is genuinely missing and reported within 48 hours of delivery:
1. Support raises a delivery issue of type "missing" against the order.
2. The customer chooses a refund of the item's paid price (credits or original method) or, where the item is in stock at their store, a free redelivery within the usual delivery time.

No photo is required for missing items.

## Repeated claims

Accounts with more than 3 missing-item claims in 30 days are routed to a human for review instead of an automatic refund (see "Fair use and refund abuse").
