## Types of credits

- **Refund credits:** from refunds, cancellations, and late delivery compensation. Valid for 12 months from the date issued.
- **Promotional credits:** from offers, referrals, and apology goodwill. Valid for 30 days unless the offer says otherwise.

The app shows each credit's expiry date in the wallet's history.

## Using credits

- Credits are applied automatically at checkout, up to the full order value. The customer can switch this off at checkout.
- Promotional credits can cover at most 50% of an order's item total.
- The oldest credits are used first.

## Rules

- Credits cannot be withdrawn to a bank account or transferred to another account.
- Expired credits cannot be restored. Support may escalate a request to a human only if credits expired because of a QuickCart error.
- When an order paid partly with credits is refunded, the credit portion goes back to the wallet, and the rest goes to the method the customer chooses.
