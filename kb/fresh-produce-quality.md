title: Fresh produce quality and weight
category: inventory
---
## Quality standard

Fruits and vegetables are checked at packing for freshness, firmness, and visible damage. Ripeness can vary; for example, bananas and mangoes may arrive slightly unripe so they last longer.

## Weight variance

Loose fruits and vegetables are sold by approximate weight. A variance of up to 10% below the listed weight is normal and is not refunded. If the delivered weight is more than 10% below the listed weight, the customer gets a refund of the shortfall when they share a photo of the item on a weighing scale within 24 hours.

## Quality complaints

Produce that is rotten, mouldy, bruised beyond normal handling, or not fit to eat is refunded or replaced under "Damaged and spoiled items", with a photo within 24 hours of delivery.

## Organic and exotic produce

Organic produce is sourced from certified suppliers and can look less uniform than regular produce. Visual differences alone are not a quality issue.
