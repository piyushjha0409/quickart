title: Non-returnable items
category: returns
---
## Items that cannot be returned once opened or used

- Personal care and hygiene products: toothbrushes, razors, cosmetics, deodorants, sanitary products.
- Innerwear and socks.
- Baby food, formula, and opened diaper packs.
- Over-the-counter medicines, health supplements, and first-aid products.
- Pet food once opened.

## Items that can never be returned

- Gift cards and QuickCart Pass subscriptions after the first 48 hours (see "QuickCart Pass membership").
- Mobile recharges and digital vouchers.
- Cut fruits, salads, and freshly prepared items.
- Items marked "Final sale" or "Non-returnable" on the product page.

## Issues are still covered

Non-returnable means no change-of-mind returns. If a non-returnable item arrives damaged, wrong, expired, or missing, it is refunded under the normal item issue policies.
