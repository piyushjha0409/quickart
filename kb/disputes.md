## When to open a dispute

A customer can open a dispute when they disagree with a support decision, for example a rejected refund, a refund for fewer items than claimed, or a charge they believe is wrong.

## How it works

1. The dispute must be opened within 7 days of the decision being disputed.
2. The customer explains what they disagree with and shares any evidence: photos, screenshots, bank statements.
3. A senior support specialist reviews the dispute, the order's evidence, and the earlier decision.
4. The customer gets a decision within 48 hours, by in-app message and email.

Opening a dispute always requires approval and review by a human. Support agents must not promise the outcome of a dispute.

## After the decision

The dispute decision is final within QuickCart. Customers can still contact the grievance officer (see "Grievance redressal").
