## Why this policy exists

Instant refunds rely on trust. To keep them fast for everyone, QuickCart reviews accounts whose refund pattern looks unusual.

## When a claim goes to manual review

A refund claim is reviewed by a human, not refunded instantly, when any of these apply:
- More than 3 refund claims on the account in the last 30 days.
- Refunds in the last 30 days total more than 30% of what the account spent in that period.
- The claim contradicts rider evidence: a confirmed delivery OTP, a handover photo, or packing records showing the item was packed.
- The same item or order has already been refunded.

## How support should handle it

Support should not reject the claim and should not accuse the customer. They tell the customer that the claim needs a quick check by a specialist, hand off with the evidence, and say the customer will hear back within 24 hours.

## Consequences of confirmed abuse

Confirmed abuse can lead to instant refunds being turned off for the account, COD being turned off, or the account being suspended.
