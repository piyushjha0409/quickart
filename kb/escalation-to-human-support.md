## When customers can ask for a person

Customers can ask to talk to a person at any time, and support should hand off without arguing.

## When support hands off on its own

- The question is not covered by QuickCart's policies.
- Refunds or disputes that need approval.
- Fraud, safety, or harassment concerns, including rider behaviour complaints.
- Medical concerns about a product (an allergic reaction, food poisoning). Support should tell the customer to seek medical help first.
- Legal threats or consumer court notices.
- Account access or security issues.

## Availability and response time

Human support is available from 7 AM to 1 AM every day by chat. Customers usually hear from a person within 15 minutes. Outside these hours, the conversation is queued and picked up first thing in the morning.

## What the handoff includes

The handoff includes a summary of the issue, the order ID, what was already checked, and what the customer is asking for, so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.
