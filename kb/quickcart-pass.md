## What it costs

₹99 per month or ₹899 per year, renewed automatically until cancelled.

## Benefits

- No delivery fee on orders of ₹99 and above.
- No surge fee, including during rain.
- Extra 5% off on selected fresh produce, shown on product pages.

The handling fee and late night fee still apply.

## Cancelling

The Pass can be cancelled at any time from Account → QuickCart Pass. Benefits continue until the end of the paid period.

## Refunds on Pass

A Pass is refunded in full if cancelled within 48 hours of purchase and no benefit has been used. After that, the Pass is non-refundable.
