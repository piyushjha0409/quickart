## Availability

Cash on delivery (COD) is available on orders up to ₹2,000. Orders above ₹2,000 must be prepaid.

## Paying at the door

The customer can pay the rider in cash or by scanning the rider's UPI QR code. Riders carry change of up to ₹500. Customers paying with a ₹2,000 note on a small order should mention it in the delivery instructions.

## Refused deliveries

Refusing a COD order at the door without a valid reason (such as a damaged or wrong order) counts as a refused delivery. Being unreachable at delivery also counts. After 2 refused deliveries in 90 days, COD is turned off for the account for 90 days. Support cannot turn it back on early.

## Refunds on COD orders

Refunds on COD orders go to QuickCart credits, or to the customer's UPI ID on request (see "Refund methods and timelines"). Cash refunds are not possible.
