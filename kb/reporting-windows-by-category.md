## Why windows differ

Fresh and frozen items change quickly after delivery, so issues with them must be reported sooner. A claim reported after its window closes is not eligible for a refund, but support may escalate genuine exceptions to a human.

## Windows

| Category | Examples | Report within | Photo needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen and ice cream | Ice cream, frozen peas, frozen snacks | 6 hours of delivery | Yes |
| Fresh produce | Fruits, vegetables, herbs, cut fruit | 24 hours of delivery | Yes, for quality issues |
| Dairy, bakery, eggs | Milk, curd, paneer, bread, eggs | 24 hours of delivery | Yes, for damage or spoilage |
| Meat and seafood | Chicken, fish, mutton | 24 hours of delivery | Yes |
| Packaged food and beverages | Atta, rice, chips, soft drinks | 48 hours of delivery | Yes, for damage |
| Home, personal care, baby care | Detergent, shampoo, diapers | 48 hours of delivery | Yes, for damage |
| Electronics and kitchenware | Chargers, bulbs, utensils | 7 days of delivery | Yes |

## Missing and wrong items

Missing items and wrong items can be reported within 48 hours of delivery for every category. No photo is needed for a missing item; a photo of the received item is needed for a wrong item.

## Expired products

Products delivered past their expiry date can be reported within 7 days of delivery, with a photo showing the expiry date. These are always refunded in full.
