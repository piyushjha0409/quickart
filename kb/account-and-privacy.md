## Account basics

A QuickCart account is linked to one mobile number, verified by OTP. Customers can add an email for invoices.

## Changing the phone number

The number can be changed from Account → Profile, which needs OTPs on both the old and new number. If the old number is no longer available, support escalates to a human for identity verification.

## Deleting an account

Customers can delete their account from Account → Privacy. Deletion is final after 30 days. Unused credits and any active QuickCart Pass are forfeited on deletion.

## Privacy

QuickCart never asks for card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, or OTPs over chat or phone. Support must never ask for these. Customers who received such a request from someone claiming to be QuickCart should report it.

## Invoices

A GST invoice for every delivered order is available in Orders → order details → Download invoice.
