## When an order is late

An order is late when it is delivered more than 10 minutes after the ETA shown at checkout. The ETA shown at checkout is the commitment; later in-app ETA updates do not change it.

## Compensation

| How late | Compensation |
| --- | --- |
| 10 to 30 minutes after checkout ETA | Delivery fee, small cart fee and surge fee refunded as credits |
| More than 30 minutes after checkout ETA | All fees above, plus ₹50 in QuickCart credits |
| Not delivered within 90 minutes | Customer may cancel for a full refund of the whole order |

Compensation is in credits only, at most once per order, and is issued after delivery. Item prices are not refunded for lateness alone.

## Exceptions

Late compensation does not apply when:
- A weather or high-demand delay notice was shown at checkout and the delivery happened within the extended ETA shown in that notice.
- The rider could not reach the customer (wrong address, phone unreachable, gated community entry refused). See "Delivery attempts and unreachable customers".
- The customer changed the address or added items after the order was placed.
