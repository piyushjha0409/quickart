## Grievance officer

Customers who are not satisfied with a support or dispute decision can write to QuickCart's grievance officer, as required under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

- Email: grievance@quickcart.example
- Acknowledgement within 48 hours.
- Resolution within 30 days of the complaint.

## What to include

The order ID, a short description of the issue, the decision being contested, and any evidence.

## National Consumer Helpline

Customers can also contact the National Consumer Helpline at 1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in.
