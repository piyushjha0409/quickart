## When a refund applies

QuickCart refunds the amount paid for an item when it is missing, wrong, damaged, spoiled, expired, or of unacceptable quality, as long as the issue is reported within the reporting window for that item's category (see "Reporting windows by item category"). Refunds are for the price actually paid for the item after discounts, not its MRP.

## What gets refunded

- **Item issues:** the paid price of the affected item(s) only. If 2 of 6 eggs arrive broken, the refund is for 2 eggs.
- **Whole order not delivered:** the full order amount, including delivery fee, handling fee, small cart fee, surge fee and rider tip.
- **Late delivery:** fees only, as credits (see "Late delivery compensation"). The item prices are not refunded for lateness alone.
- **Coupons:** when an order used a coupon, the coupon discount is shared across items in proportion to their price, and the refund for an item is its share of what was actually paid.

## Refund destination

Customers can choose between QuickCart credits and the original payment method. See "Refund methods and timelines".

## Approval

Small refunds to QuickCart credits are issued instantly by support. Larger refunds, and any refund to the original payment method, go through a quick review by the support team, usually completed within 4 hours between 8 AM and 11 PM. Support agents must not promise a refund outcome before that review is done.

## Refunds are not given for

- Change of mind on perishable items (fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat, frozen food).
- Items listed as non-returnable once opened or used (see "Non-returnable items").
- Issues reported after the reporting window has closed.
- Claims contradicted by delivery evidence (see "Order marked delivered but not received").
