## Accepted payment methods

UPI, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, third-party wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay), QuickCart credits, and cash on delivery for eligible orders. Credits can be combined with any other method.

## Money deducted but order not placed

Sometimes a payment succeeds at the bank but the order fails to be created. In that case the amount is reversed automatically to the same payment method within 5 to 7 business days for cards and net banking, and within 48 hours for UPI. No action is needed from the customer. Support can confirm the reversal status using the payment reference.

## Charged twice

If one order was charged twice, the duplicate charge is detected and reversed automatically within the same timelines. If it has not been reversed after 7 business days, support escalates to the payments team with the transaction IDs.

## Payment pending

UPI payments can show "Pending" for up to 10 minutes. The order is placed only after the payment is confirmed. If it stays pending, the money is reversed automatically.
