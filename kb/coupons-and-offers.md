## Using coupons

- Only one coupon can be used per order.
- Each coupon has a minimum order value, a maximum discount, and an expiry date, shown on the coupon.
- Coupons are applied to the item total, not to fees.
- First-order coupons are valid only for the first order placed on an account and phone number.

## Coupons and refunds

- If the whole order is cancelled or not delivered, a coupon that has not expired is restored to the account.
- For a refund of some items, the refund is the item's share of the amount actually paid after the coupon (see "Refund policy overview"). The coupon is not restored.
- If a partial refund brings the order below the coupon's minimum order value, the remaining discount is not taken back.

## Coupon did not apply

If a valid coupon failed to apply at checkout, support can issue the missed discount as promotional credits after checking the coupon's terms. Support must not offer discounts or credits that the coupon terms do not cover.
