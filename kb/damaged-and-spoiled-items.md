## Covered issues

- Packaging torn, crushed, or leaking (for example a leaking milk pouch or a crushed chips packet).
- Broken items such as eggs, glass jars, or bottles.
- Spoiled or poor-quality fresh items: bruised, rotten, or overripe produce; sour milk or curd; stale bread; frozen items that arrived melted.

## What the customer needs to share

A clear photo of the damaged item, reported within the window for its category (see "Reporting windows by item category"). For broken eggs, the photo should show the tray so the number of broken eggs is visible.

## Resolution

- Refund of the paid price of the damaged units only (for example 2 broken eggs out of 6, not the whole tray), unless the whole item is unusable (a leaking oil bottle that has contaminated the bag counts as fully damaged).
- Or a free replacement if the item is in stock at the customer's store.
- Leaked liquids that damage other items in the same bag: the other damaged items are refunded too, with photos.

## Melted frozen items

Ice cream and frozen food that arrive melted or soft are refunded in full if reported within 6 hours of delivery with a photo.

The customer does not need to return damaged items. They should dispose of them safely.
