## Free cancellation

An order can be cancelled for free until the store starts packing it, usually within 60 to 90 seconds of placing it. The cancel button in the app disappears once packing starts.

## After packing has started

- **Packing in progress or packed, not yet picked up by the rider:** support can cancel the order. The item amount is refunded in full; the delivery fee and handling fee are kept as a cancellation charge.
- **Out for delivery:** the order cannot be cancelled, except in the case below.

## Cancelling a late order

If the order has not been delivered within 90 minutes of being placed, the customer may cancel it for a full refund, including all fees and tip, at any stage.

## Cancellations by QuickCart

If QuickCart cancels an order (for example, the store closes unexpectedly or all items are out of stock), the customer gets a full refund to the original payment method, and support may add ₹25 in credits as an apology.

## Refund of a cancelled prepaid order

Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders go back to the original payment method automatically, on the timelines in "Refund methods and timelines". Customers can ask support to send it as credits instead.
