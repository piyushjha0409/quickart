## What to check first

When the app says "Delivered" but the customer has nothing, support checks the rider evidence on the order:
- **Delivery OTP:** whether the customer's 4-digit OTP was entered.
- **Delivery photo:** whether the rider took a doorstep or handover photo.
- **Rider GPS:** whether the rider was within 100 metres of the delivery address at the time of delivery.

Support should also ask the customer to check with family, neighbours, the building's security desk, and any doorstep the rider may have used.

## Resolution

- **No OTP, no photo, or rider GPS more than 100 metres away:** full refund of the order, including fees and tip.
- **OTP entered or handover photo present:** support must not refund directly. The case is escalated to a human with the evidence for investigation, normally resolved within 24 hours. Support should stay polite and not accuse the customer.

## Reporting window

Must be reported within 24 hours of the order being marked delivered.
