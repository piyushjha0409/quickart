## Fees on every order

| Fee | Amount | When it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery fee | ₹25 | Item total below ₹199. Free at ₹199 and above |
| Small cart fee | ₹15 | Item total below ₹99 |
| Handling fee | ₹4 | Every order |
| Late night fee | ₹15 | Orders placed between 11 PM and 6 AM |
| Surge fee | ₹10 to ₹30 | Heavy rain or very high demand; shown before checkout |

All fees are shown on the checkout screen before payment. QuickCart never adds a fee after the order is placed.

## QuickCart Pass

Pass members pay no delivery fee on orders of ₹99 and above, and no surge fee. The handling fee and late night fee still apply (see "QuickCart Pass membership").

## Refunds of fees

Fees are refunded when the whole order is cancelled before packing or not delivered, and as credits under late delivery compensation. Fees are not refunded when only some items in an order have issues.

## Prices

Item prices are never higher than MRP. The price charged is the price shown at checkout, even if it changes afterwards.
