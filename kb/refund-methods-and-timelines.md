---
title: Refund methods and timelines
category: refunds
---
## Refund to QuickCart credits

Credits are added to the customer's QuickCart wallet instantly once the refund is approved. Credits can be used on the next order straight away. This is the fastest option and support should offer it first, while making clear the customer may choose the original payment method instead.

## Refund to the original payment method

Once approved, the refund is sent back to the method used to pay. Typical time for the money to reach the customer after approval:

| Payment method | Timeline |
| --- | --- |
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Debit or credit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 3 to 5 business days |
| Third-party wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay) | 1 to 3 business days |

Timelines depend on the bank after QuickCart releases the refund. If the money has not arrived after the maximum timeline, the customer should share their bank statement and support will raise a trace with the payment partner using the refund reference number.

## Cash on delivery orders

COD orders cannot be refunded to cash. The customer can choose QuickCart credits (instant) or a transfer to their own UPI ID (1 to 3 business days after approval).

## Refund reference

Every refund has a refund ID. Support quotes only the refund ID returned by the refund system and never makes one up.
