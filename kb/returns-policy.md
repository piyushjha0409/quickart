## Change of mind returns

Customers can return unopened, unused packaged items for a refund within 7 days of delivery. The item must be in its original sealed packaging with the label intact.

## What cannot be returned for change of mind

- All perishables: fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, eggs, meat, seafood, frozen food, fresh flowers.
- Items on the non-returnable list (see "Non-returnable items").

Perishables can still be refunded when they are damaged, spoiled, wrong, missing, or expired, under the item issue policies.

## Defective electronics and kitchenware

Electronics and kitchenware that are defective or stop working within 7 days of delivery can be returned for a refund or replacement, even if opened. After 7 days, the manufacturer's warranty applies and the customer should contact the brand.

## How returns work

1. Support raises a return request against the order.
2. A rider collects the item within 24 hours, at a time slot the customer picks. There is no pickup charge.
3. The refund is approved after the rider confirms the item is unopened (or defective, for electronics) at pickup.

If the item fails the pickup check, the return is cancelled and the item stays with the customer.
