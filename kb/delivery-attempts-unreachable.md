title: Delivery attempts and unreachable customers
category: delivery
---
## What the rider does

If the customer is not reachable on arrival, the rider:
1. Calls the customer twice through the app's masked calling.
2. Waits at the address for 5 minutes.
3. Contacts QuickCart support, who try the customer once more.

## If the customer still cannot be reached

The order is returned to the store and marked "Undelivered — customer unreachable".
- **Prepaid orders:** perishable items are not refunded; packaged items are refunded to QuickCart credits. Fees and tip are not refunded.
- **COD orders:** nothing is charged, but the account gets a refused delivery mark (see "Cash on delivery").

## Wrong or incomplete address

The customer is responsible for the delivery address and pin location. The address can be changed in the app until the rider picks up the order, if the new address is within the same store's delivery area. Late delivery compensation does not apply when the delay was caused by a wrong or incomplete address.

## Gated societies

Customers in gated communities should approve the rider at the security gate (via apps such as MyGate) promptly. Delays at the gate caused by pending approval are not counted as late delivery.
