## How fast QuickCart delivers

Most orders are delivered in about 10 minutes from the customer's nearest dark store. The ETA shown at checkout is calculated from the store's current load, rider availability, and distance, and is usually between 8 and 20 minutes.

## How an order moves

1. **Placed:** payment confirmed.
2. **Packing:** the store team picks and packs the order, usually within 2 minutes.
3. **Out for delivery:** a rider has picked up the order. Live tracking starts.
4. **Delivered:** handed over and confirmed by delivery OTP or photo.

## Delays

The ETA can be longer during heavy rain, festivals, late night, or unusually high demand. When this is expected, the app shows a delay notice and an extended ETA at checkout. If a delay starts after the order is placed, the customer is notified in the app.

Lateness is measured against the ETA shown at checkout (see "Late delivery compensation").
