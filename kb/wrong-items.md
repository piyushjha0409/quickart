## What counts as a wrong item

The delivered item is a different product, variant, brand, or pack size from the one ordered, and it was not an accepted substitution. Examples: toned milk delivered instead of full cream; a 500 g pack instead of 1 kg; a different brand of atta.

A substitution the customer opted into is not a wrong item (see "Substitution policy"), but the customer can still reject a substitute.

## What the customer needs to share

A photo of the item they received, reported within 48 hours of delivery.

## Resolution

1. Support raises a delivery issue of type "wrong item" against the order.
2. The customer gets a refund of the ordered item's paid price, or a free redelivery of the correct item where it is in stock.
3. The customer may keep the wrong item. QuickCart does not collect wrong items of value under ₹500. For wrong items of ₹500 or more, the rider collects the item when delivering the correct one, or within 48 hours.
