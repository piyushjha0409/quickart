## When substitutions happen

Stock at a dark store can run out between checkout and packing. When an ordered item is out of stock at packing, what happens depends on the customer's substitution preference, chosen at checkout:

- **Substitute with a similar item** (default): the packer replaces it with the same product in a different pack size or brand, or a close equivalent.
- **Call me first:** the packer calls the customer. If the customer does not answer within 60 seconds, the item is removed.
- **Don't substitute:** the item is removed.

Removed items are never charged; the amount is not taken, or is reversed automatically.

## Rules for substitutes

- A substitute is never charged at more than the price of the original item. If the substitute costs more, the customer pays the original price.
- If the substitute costs less, the customer pays the lower price and the difference is reversed automatically.
- Substitutes must be the same category and dietary type (for example, vegetarian for vegetarian, sugar-free for sugar-free).
- The order screen and invoice mark every substituted item.

## Rejecting a substitute

The customer can reject a substitute at the door by handing it back to the rider, or report it within 24 hours of delivery through support. A rejected substitute is refunded in full and the customer does not have to return it if it is under ₹200.
