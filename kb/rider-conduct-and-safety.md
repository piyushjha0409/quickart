---
title: Rider conduct and safety
category: support
---
## Expected rider conduct

Riders should be polite, wear their QuickCart ID, hand over the order at the door (or the security gate if the customer asks), and never ask for extra money beyond the COD amount.

## Reporting a rider

Customers can report rude behaviour, asking for extra money, unsafe driving, or harassment. Support always escalates these reports to the safety team, and never resolves them with a refund alone. The customer's contact details are never shared with the rider.

## Emergencies

For any threat to personal safety, the customer should call 112 first. The QuickCart safety team can be reached through the in-app SOS button on any active order.

## Tips

Tips are optional and go 100% to the rider. A tip is refunded only if the order is cancelled or not delivered.
